# Stochastic vs deterministic Samudra at 4deg -- the screening arms of
# tasks/2026-08-26-stochastic-samudra-4deg-screen.
#
# All four arms share train-config.yaml (the 4deg port of the 1deg
# ocean_wide_ohc_clip recipe) and differ only in the override_args below, so
# the re-measured deterministic control is comparable to the stochastic arms
# arm for arm. n_forward_steps stays at the config's 4 and batch_size at 16
# (global) throughout, per the task's 2026-08-26 decision-log entry.
#
# 4 GPUs rather than the rest of the 4deg protocol's 2, as requested: the
# stochastic arms' n_ensemble=2 doubles the effective per-GPU batch, and 4 GPUs
# keeps the deterministic control on the same footprint as the arms it is the
# control for. batch_size is global, so all four arms train at the same 730
# iterations/epoch as the 1deg protocol.
#
# The stochastic arms take DLESyM-Ocean's settings rather than sweeping:
# n_ensemble 2, noise_embed_dim 32 (the NoiseConditionedSamudra default),
# noise_injection bottleneck, patch size 3x3. The spectral energy score stays
# off (energy_score_weight 0) -- an SHT over a land-masked field puts spurious
# power at high degree exactly where the whitening reweight amplifies it.
#
# uncoupled_train.sh acts only on rows whose status is exactly "train". Every
# row below is staged at "skip" pending review.
#
# Launch with:
#   job_runner/uncoupled_train.sh \
#     configs/experiments/cm4_1pct_46to125_piC_156to235_4deg/ocean_wide_stochastic . \
#     --coupled_stats elynn/2026-07-22-cm4-1pctco2-4deg-coupled-stats
