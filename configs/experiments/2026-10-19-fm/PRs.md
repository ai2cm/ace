## Anneal sweep

30 fine-tuning runs (6 arms x 5 seeds) from the balanced nc-sfno parents'
`ema_ckpt_0150.tar`, wandb group `ace2-fm-anneal-2026-10-19`. Configs, arms,
deviations, log keys, smoke results and Beaker ids:
[run_configs/anneal/README.md](run_configs/anneal/README.md). Generated by
`create_anneal_jobs.py`, submitted by `submit_anneal_jobs.py`.
Post-processing (README "Post-processing"): `make_soup_checkpoints.py`
(checkpoint C, soup of `ema_ckpt_0016..0020` -> Beaker `{run}-ckptC`,
`anneal_soups.json`), `submit_anneal_eval_jobs.py` (J1/J3/J4 for checkpoints
C, B, P), `run_anneal_postprocess.sh` (map refresh + soups + evals, run once
training is done).

## FM v2 runs (2026-11-05)

20 training runs from scratch, wandb group `ace2-fm-2026-11-05` (ai2cm/FM):
2 architectures (`nc-sfno-v2`: SFNO embed_dim 1024; `nc-swin-v2.2-v2`: Swin
embed_dim 512) x 2 data arms x 5 seeds, masking off, 4 GPUs (global batch 8).
Jobs are `ace2-fm-nc-{sfno,swin-v2.2}-v2-fm-{balanced,e3sm-balanced}-s{0..4}-maskoff`.
`fm-balanced` reuses the balanced parents' data and v2 stats; `fm-e3sm-balanced`
adds E3SMv3 piControl (v3 stats), c96 AMIP ic_0002 and c96 SOM ic_0002..0005
(except held-out 1xCO2-ic_0005). 100 epochs with a 1/2/4-step curriculum
(milestones 41, 71), LR 1e-4 constant to epoch 70 then linear to 0, EMA 0.9999
(checkpoints every 10 epochs), inline inference aimip ERA5 every epoch and the
ERA5 10yr/46yr runs every 10. Generated by `create_fm_v2_jobs.py` (its
docstring lists every change from the parents), submitted by
`submit_fm_v2_jobs.py`; smoke configs in `run_configs/v2-smoke/`. Progress
rows in `fm_table.py`.

CUDA-graph fix and resubmission. The first v2 submission ran SFNO with
`FME_COMPILE_MODE=default` (no CUDA graphs) because under fme's default
`reduce-overhead` mode the SFNO smoke failed at the 2-step milestone in
backward with "accessing tensor output of CUDAGraphs that has been overwritten
by a subsequent run": gradients accumulated across forward steps stayed in the
CUDA-graph memory pool and were overwritten by the next replay. PR #1610 fixes
this by cloning accumulated grads out of the CUDA-graph pools (commits
71380e58f, 6506032be); `origin/feat/compile-cudagraphs` at 6506032be is merged
into this branch. The per-arch override was then removed, so both
architectures run `reduce-overhead`, both smokes were rerun under the merged
code, the first-submission v2 jobs (running unfixed code) were stopped, and all
20 jobs were resubmitted (Beaker names carry a hash suffix; `fm_table.py`
strips it).
